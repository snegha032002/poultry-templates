How our hens live

Good eggs come from hens that are looked after.

Healthy hens lay stronger, tastier eggs. This is how we care for ours every day.

Clean, airy sheds

Open-sided sheds with cross ventilation, cleaned and disinfected on a fixed schedule.

Balanced feed

Our own feed mix of maize, soya and calcium grit for strong shells and rich yolks.

Clean water, all day

Filtered water through nipple drinkers in every shed, checked twice a day.

Weekly vet visits

A poultry vet checks the flock every week. No antibiotics are used to boost laying.
