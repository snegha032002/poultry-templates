A day at Golden Nest.

Scroll from night to morning. Every egg in your box went through this day.

  1. 05:00

    Lights on in the sheds

    The hens wake up, eat and drink. Most eggs are laid in the next three hours.

    Laying sheds
  2. 06:30

    First collection

    Our team collects eggs by hand from every row. A second round follows at noon.

    Collection
  3. 09:00

    Cleaned, candled, graded

    Shells are dry cleaned, checked over a light for cracks and sorted by weight.

    Grading room
  4. 14:00

    Packed and dated

    Every box is stamped with the day it was laid and the shed it came from.

    Packing
  5. 06:00

    Next morning, at your door

    Our vans leave before sunrise. Your eggs arrive before 8 AM.
